Enterprise Sales & Finance Semantic Model

Impact
One source of truth for every UK region, all sales channels, Finance and leadership. Contract-health reporting flagged £5M+ (~$6.5M) in annualized contract revenue pending rollover, and ~13,500 manual requests a year were eliminated (~$52K). Nominated for Biffa's Diamond Awards.
Problem & approach
Problem
Sales and Finance ran on 5+ siloed Excel processes (pipeline, revenue projection, rolling revenue, churn, contract health), with competing revenue definitions, including the ones used for bonuses.
Approach
A galaxy-schema semantic model: contract, revenue and service facts from the central system, customer and CRM data from NetSuite, and HR data from Dayforce, with one agreed set of revenue definitions, row-level security and deployment pipelines. I managed the project end to end with the Sales and Finance business owners, from requirements through development and adoption.

Revenue method: of the £5M+ flagged, roughly 30% went to the business as recoverable revenue and about 70% was obsolete contracts that were suspended (a data-quality clean-up); the split is an estimate. Savings method: ~13,500 report requests a year × 6 minutes each × $39/hour average cost of the people requesting and processing them ≈ $52K (£40K) a year. BAT (British American Tobacco) · 2022 – 2025

Change Request Selection Tool

↑200+ hrssaved per month
↓5 → 1 minper request
↓~10%fewer incorrect CRs
Impact
Selection time cut from ~5 to ~1 minute (200+ hours saved a month) and ~10% fewer incorrect requests; 3K+ views a month across five continents.
Problem & approach
Problem
Global operations users raised 3,000+ SKU change requests a month and had to find the right type among 200+ CR types, taking ~5 minutes each, with wrong picks causing rework.
Approach
A report with slicers for change type, data object and market that filters 200+ CR types into a table, highlights the best match and links straight to the form to raise it, following global master-data rules.

Hours method: 3,000+ requests a month × ~4 minutes saved each ≈ 200 hours a month. Master-data governance rules live in the filters, so process adherence is enforced by design rather than by review. The interface shown is a simplified recreation with sample values.
